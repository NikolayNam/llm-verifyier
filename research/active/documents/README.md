# Research Documents

This directory stores active research documents and experiment-family writeups.

English files at this level are the canonical operator-facing sources. Manual
Russian companion copies, when present, live under `documents/ru/`.

Use `documents/` for:

- benchmark-family specifications
- research hypotheses and conclusions
- experiment-family status documents
- interpretation and cleanup layers that constrain how active experiment
  results may be read
- operator runbooks for active research phases when those commands should be
  versioned and reviewable
- active cross-phase research execution plans when a benchmark family needs an
  explicit next-cycle order or decision tree
- phase-specific experiment expansion plans when a clean starter surface must
  remain distinct from a richer annotated follow-on
- bridge/import/final transition diagnostics when a compositional lane needs a
  narrower operator-facing failure taxonomy
- split bridge-only or final-only experiment designs when a mixed
  compositional lane must be decomposed into cleaner causal surfaces
- kernel-parity or equivalence documents when a replicated checker must be
  compared against the current approved verifier on a fixed corpus

Do not place prompt registries or coordination matrices here. Use:

- `../prompts/`
- `../matrix/`
