# Active Research

This subtree stores active, non-canonical research materials that inform later
architecture, technical-spec, and product decisions.

The active research space is intentionally split by purpose:

- `documents/`
  - research documents, benchmark writeups, experiment-family narratives, and
    versioned operator runbooks
- `prompts/`
  - prompt registries and prompt-family notes for research benchmark runners
- `matrix/`
  - cross-track status matrices and guardrail coordination tables

Use this split to avoid mixing:

- benchmark documents
- prompt-profile notes
- cross-track coordination matrices

Repository-facing technical specifications remain under:

- `docs/technical-specs/`
